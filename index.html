Happy birthday, Excy.

Happy birthday, Excy. I’m really proud of what you have become and how much you’ve changed. I’ve seen all the changes you’ve made, and I really hope you keep it up. I want you to know that I’ll always be here, supporting you, loving you, caring for you, and being proud of you.

There are also so many little things about you that I’ll always remember. Like how whenever someone asks you for something or wants to borrow something from you, you’ll pause and wait for them to say “please” and “thank you.” HAHAHA. Or how when you sneeze, you’ll wait for someone to say “bless you.” Those things might seem so small, pero those are the things that make you, you. You’ve even taught me to be more mindful about saying thank you, and honestly, I’m glad you did.

I hope you enjoy your day and spend it with the people who make you happy. Deserve kaayo nimo ma-enjoy imong birthday. Sana you get to laugh a lot, eat good food, receive the things you’ve been hoping for, and make memories that you’ll remember for a long time.

I’m really happy that I get to see you grow and become the person you are today. I’ve noticed the changes in you, even the small ones, and I hope you keep becoming the person you want to be. Padayon lang gyud, even when things get difficult. You don’t have to figure everything out at once. Take your time and enjoy where you are right now.

I hope you never lose those little parts of yourself that make you who you are. Your little habits, the way you care about things, the way you express yourself, and even the random things you do that you probably don’t think much about. Those are the things people remember about you.

And I hope all of your 11:11 wishes come true. Sana matupad tanan nimong gipangandoy, even the wishes you keep to yourself. I hope this year gives you more happiness, more memories, and more reasons to smile. I hope you get closer to the things you want and that you always have people around you who genuinely care about you.

Thank you for all the little things you’ve taught me, even the things you probably didn’t realize you were teaching me. Thank you for the memories, the laughs, and all the small moments that I still remember. I’m really glad I got to know you and see the person you’ve become.

Happy birthday again, Excy. Enjoy your day, okay? Amping always, and please take care of yourself. I hope this year is good to you and gives you plenty of reasons to be happy. I’ll always be proud of you.

— always cheering for you ❤️
